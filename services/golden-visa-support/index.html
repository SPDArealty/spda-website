Golden Visa support

We select property that meets the investment threshold and guide you through the application.

Talk to a founderWhatsApp

What we do

  1. 01
    Threshold

    One or more properties with a total value of at least AED 2 million (about USD 545,000), fully owned and registered in your name.

  2. 02
    Financing

    A mortgage from an approved local bank is accepted. The Dubai Land Department asks for a bank no-objection letter showing the amount paid and the balance.

  3. 03
    Residency

    10 years, renewable, without a sponsor. Spouse, children and parents can be sponsored.

Conditions and process

  • A lien is placed on the property for the validity of the residency, and the property cannot be sold during that period. Valid health insurance is required.
  • Off-plan: the ICP guide lists units bought from an approved company. The Dubai Land Department service page does not describe this route, so each case needs checking.
  • Process: documents, submission and fees at an accredited centre, medical examination, permit by email. The Dubai Land Department lists 7 to 10 business days and AED 9,884.75 in government fees (about USD 2,690).

Sources: ICP Golden Residency Guide (updated 10 Sep 2026), Dubai Land Department Golden Visa application page and GDRFA golden residence page, read 4 Nov 2026. The authorities decide each application.
